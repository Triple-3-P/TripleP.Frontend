# Landing backdrop

`mountain-backdrop.webp` is decorative imagery generated with the built-in imagegen tool, then encoded as WebP at quality 85. It contains no product UI or text. Next.js Image handles responsive delivery; an empty alt attribute keeps the decoration out of the accessibility tree.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: decorative wide landing page backdrop. A cinematic black and white photograph of dramatic alpine peaks emerging from low clouds. Dark charcoal mountains on the left, silvery mist and pale sky on the right, calm and atmospheric, fine natural rock texture and subtle film grain. Wide landscape composition, central negative space where a product UI will be overlaid later. Strict monochrome, understated premium editorial aesthetic, no people, no buildings, no graphics, no text, no logo, no watermark.
